How the flags are made

Each flag is drawn by the game itself, shape by shape: stripes, crosses, stars, crescents and discs, cut into a circle. That is why the flags stay sharp on any screen, from a small phone to a large monitor. The color of your territory in the arena comes from the main color of your flag.
