About Country Clash

Country Clash is a free browser game where countries fight for space. Pick your flag, shoot your balls, cut the arena and take territory before the clock runs out.

Where it comes from

Country Clash started on TikTok, on the account @country_clashh. Every day, the account posts short simulations: two countries, three flag balls each, bouncing around an arena and cutting it into pieces. The last country standing wins.

Thousands of people watched those matches and kept asking the same thing in the comments: “Can I play this?” This website is the answer. The game uses the same rules as the videos, but now you control the shots.

What makes it different

  • Short matches. One match takes less than a minute, so you can play one more, and then one more.
  • Turn by turn. No fast fingers needed. You aim, you shoot, then your rival plays. Strategy beats speed.
  • 58 countries. Every flag is drawn as a round ball, from Turkey to Brazil, from Japan to Morocco.
  • Free, no account. Open the page and play, on a phone or a computer.

Who makes it

Country Clash is made by Bazil Falah, a graphic designer based in France who creates the TikTok series and designs the game. Ideas from players shape every update: the slow motion on the final cut, the country picker and the five-second turns all came from comments.

Fair play

Country Clash is about friendly rivalry. The game never takes sides: every match is decided by the players' shots, and the rival country is picked at random. We keep politics out of the game, and some pairs of countries are never matched against each other.
