Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 2 November 2026

Country Clash is a free browser game. You can play without an account, and we never ask for your email address or phone number. If you want to appear in the ranking, you choose a nickname. This page explains the little data the game uses and the cookies set by our advertising partner.

Who we are

Country Clash (country-clash.online) is run by Bazil Falah. For any question about this policy or your data, write to bazil.falah@gmail.com.

What the game collects

To improve the game, we record anonymous statistics about each match:

  • when the match started and how long it lasted;
  • the result (won, lost, draw or left before the end), the share of the arena held by each side and the number of turns played;
  • the arena and background color of the match;
  • whether the game was played on a phone or a computer.

These statistics are linked to a random match number only. They contain no name, no contact details and no precise location, and we do not use them to identify you. They are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

Ranking

When you win against the computer, the game sends your win to our ranking server (Supabase) so that it counts for your country. The ranking only stores the name you chose and your country, with the points and the time of each win, linked to a random player number kept in your browser. No account and no real name are needed: choose a nickname, not your full name. Names and countries are shown publicly on the Ranking page. To remove your name from the ranking, write to bazil.falah@gmail.com.

Your name and trophies

Each ranking name belongs to one player. Your name is remembered in your browser, so you keep it the next time you play. A hidden code, stored encrypted on our server and in your browser, protects your name so nobody else can take it. Your trophy progress (number of wins, trails broken…) is saved with your name.

Your e-mail (only if you give it)

After a win, the game may offer you to leave your e-mail address to receive the mobile app first and a golden crown for your flag ball. This is optional. If you give it, we store your e-mail with your ranking name and country, and we only use it to tell you about Country Clash (for example when the app is released). We never sell it or share it with advertisers. It is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until the app is released plus 12 months. You can unsubscribe or ask for deletion at any time by writing to bazil.falah@gmail.com. If you are under 15, ask a parent before giving your e-mail.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and matches and to see how the game is used (for example which arenas are played most, or whether people play on a phone or a computer). Google Analytics uses cookies and receives technical information such as your browser type and approximate region. We do not send your name or contact details to Google. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

What is saved on your device

The game saves a few small settings in your browser's local storage: your country, mode and level, whether you have seen the short tutorial, and, if you play for the ranking, your ranking name and random player number. Only the ranking name, player number and country are sent with your wins. You can remove them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising and cookies

We show ads to keep the game free. Ads are served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads and their cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings link shown with the consent message. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Hosting

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) for security and to keep the site running. These logs are kept for a short period and are not used to identify you.

Children

Country Clash is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. Because the game statistics are anonymous, we usually cannot link them to you, but you can contact us at bazil.falah@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example when the mobile app is released. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
